## Top 3 source problems

1. **EN recommended factors used instead of the UK National Annex** (pile
   sheet). R4 = 1.4 against UK NA 2.0 (bored/CFA, no SLS verification), ξ =
   1.27 against 1.39, and no model factor 1.4. Results are unconservative by
   roughly 2×.
2. **Checks that cannot fail.** The track mat bending check tests an empty
   cell, so it always shows PASS. The EKKI mat shear check uses a moment as
   a shear force. The track mat compression check compares an SLS stress
   with a ULS stress.
3. **Partial factor on the angle, not on tan φ′** (EKKI, Track Mat, Unified
   Crane, EC Pad). φ′/1.25 instead of tan⁻¹(tan φ′/1.25). Also Meyerhof Nγ
   (tan 1.4φ) used in place of EC7 Annex D Nγ.
