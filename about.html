About this toolkit

Purpose

This site hosts a growing library of engineering calculation tools for the internal engineering team at Beaver Bridges Ltd, used to derive design calculation information — quick, consistent, checkable working. Every calculation must be independently reviewed and signed off by a Chartered Engineer before it is accepted for use.

Methodology and scope

Each calculation states its own references, assumptions, and scope directly on its page and on its printed calculation sheet. All calculations use metric units and are based on UK design codes (Eurocodes with UK National Annexes, DMRB, and Network Rail / client-specific standards where relevant). Where a calculation uses a simplified method, or a method's stated scope of validity is limited, this is stated explicitly in its "Assumptions" panel.

Mandatory verification

Every output from this toolkit is advisory. Nothing produced here may be used for tender, fabrication, or construction without independent verification and sign-off by a Chartered Engineer (CEng MICE / MIStructE). CDM 2015 design responsibilities — including designing out hazards during fabrication, erection, maintenance and demolition — remain with the responsible designer at every stage, regardless of what this toolkit reports.

Validation

Every calculation ships with at least one validation sample — a known input/output pair used to sanity-check the implementation. These run on a hidden validation page, intended for contributors checking a change to a calculation, not for day-to-day engineering use.

Contributing

To add a new calculation, see CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository. New calculations must reference UK/EC design standards and include the CEng verification statement.
